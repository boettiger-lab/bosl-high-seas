The map shows the daily anomaly from NOAA Coral Reef Watch's 5 km product, measured against the CRW climatology for 1985–1990 and 1993. It plays every fourth day of the last 13 months at 0.2° resolution, read directly from the files published on NRP. The dashed box (20–60°N, 180–110°W) is the region that the two figures above and the chart describe, and the dots mark Kāneʻohe Bay and Puakō. The data are from NOAA Coral Reef Watch and are in the public domain.
